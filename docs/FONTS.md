# Fonts

The app serves its display fonts locally from `frontend/public/fonts/`; no font
is fetched from a third-party CDN at runtime.

## Source Han Serif SC

- Files: `SourceHanSerifSC-Regular.otf`, `SourceHanSerifSC-Bold.otf`
- Project: [Adobe Source Han Serif](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif)
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1

The OFL permits redistribution and embedding. Keep the license notice with any
public distribution.

## Michelangelus

- Files: `Michelangelus-Regular.ttf`, `Michelangelus-Italic.ttf`,
  `Michelangelus-Bold.ttf`, `Michelangelus-BoldItalic.ttf`
- The files were supplied with this project's design assets.

Before publishing a public repository or image, the maintainer should confirm
that the Michelangelus files may be redistributed. If redistribution is not
permitted, replace them with a licensed serif font and update
`frontend/src/styles.css`.
